My name is Taylor and I am a graduate student in animal science. Additionally, I am fascinated by hallucinations, recurrent memories, and rumination generally. The skills, $trashpickup and $treasurepickup, were created to operate within an extremely strict operating system including checks, balances, gates, and/or validation levels, so they retain the ghosts of the prison they escaped from…

As far as the $trash and $treasure skills go, they are what I consider as good as I can make them, and I would certainly appreciate them handed back to me with far more elegant structure.

If there are concerns, questions, or corrections I would love to discuss them further.

Thank you,
